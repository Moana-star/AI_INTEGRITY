Risk Analysis

Explainable rule-based decision support for examination staff.

AI-assisted decision support: This prototype applies explainable predefined rules to recorded incidents. It does not automatically detect cheating or determine guilt.

How risk is determined

High-severity incident recorded?Yes - Critical
Two or more incidents, with no high-severity incident?Yes - High
At least one incident, and neither rule above applies?Yes - Watch
No incident record?No flag
Human review: Risk flags support invigilators and administrators during review. They are not proof of misconduct, and the system makes no disciplinary decision automatically. Final decisions remain with authorized examination staff.
